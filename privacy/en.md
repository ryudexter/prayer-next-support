# Privacy Policy of Prayer Next

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Prayer Next collects no Personal Data. It shows no advertising, uses no analytics or tracking services, has no accounts, and sends no Data to servers run by the Owner or anyone else.

## What stays on the User's devices

* **Location:** when the User chooses Current Location, Prayer Next asks iOS for the device's location and uses it on the device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. The User can instead choose a city from a list built into the app. The location or city is kept on the device and is never sent to the Owner.
* **Settings:** the User's chosen city, calculation method, Asr time, appearance and notification choices. They are kept on the iPhone or iPad, shared with Prayer Next's own widgets on the same device, and sent to the User's own Apple Watch so that it shows the same times.
* **Notifications:** prayer reminders are scheduled on the device by iOS. They are off until the User turns one on.

## Not collected

Prayer Next does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), cookies or similar technologies, and does not track Users across other companies' apps or websites.

## Support

If the User emails the Owner, the Owner receives that email and the User's email address, and uses them only to reply.

## Changes to this policy

The Owner will post any change to this policy on this page and update the date above.
