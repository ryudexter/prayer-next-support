# Prayer Next

Today's prayer times and a countdown to the next one, on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and in widgets. It needs iOS 17 or watchOS 10 or later.

This page is Prayer Next's support page. The app's code isn't here.

Prayer Next is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking. See the [privacy policy](privacy/en.md).

## Getting help

Email **[zcengel@yahoo.com](mailto:zcengel@yahoo.com)**. Say which city you're in, which calculation method Prayer Next shows in Settings, and which time looks wrong and by how much.

## Common questions

**The times are a few minutes off from my mosque's timetable.** Mosques and countries use different calculation methods. Prayer Next picks the usual method for your country, and you can choose another in Settings › Calculation Method. If Asr is about an hour off, change Settings › Asr Time between Shafi'i and Hanafi.

**It shows the wrong city.** Open Settings › Location and pick Current Location, or search for your city. If you chose Current Location, check that Prayer Next is allowed to use your location in the iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

**I don't get notifications.** Notifications are off until you turn one on. Turn on the prayers you want in Prayer Next's Settings › Notifications, and allow notifications when iOS asks. If you said no earlier, turn them on in the iPhone's Settings › Notifications › Prayer Next.

**How do I add a widget?** Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, and search for Prayer Next. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, then Lock Screen, and tap the widget area.

**Can I have it on my Mac?** Yes, as a desktop widget. With macOS Sonoma or later and your iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account, right-click the Mac's desktop, choose Edit Widgets, and find Prayer Next under your iPhone's widgets.

**The Qibla compass points the wrong way.** Hold the iPhone flat, away from metal and magnets, and move it in a figure eight to calibrate the compass.

**How do I get it on my Apple Watch?** Install Prayer Next on the iPhone, then open the Watch app on the iPhone, tap My Watch, and install Prayer Next under Available Apps. To put the next prayer on a watch face, edit the face and choose Prayer Next for a complication.
